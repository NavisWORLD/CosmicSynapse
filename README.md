# COSMIC SYNAPSE

**Cory Shane Davis · independent builder, researcher, artist, writer, musician, and creator**

I build because I love creating.

Sometimes that means software. Sometimes music, painting, writing, electronics, simulations, educational tools, AI systems, scientific research, or questions that do not fit neatly inside one field.

At the center of much of this work is **Cosmic Synapse Theory (CST)**: a computational research program exploring persistent multidimensional state, memory, recurrence, coupling, learned association, signal-driven dynamics, and ways of turning strange ideas into systems that can be inspected, measured, challenged, and improved.

CST is not a demand that anyone believe a cosmic story. The strongest version of the work separates what is **implemented**, what is **measured**, what remains a **hypothesis**, and what is only a **model or metaphor**.

## Why I share

I believe knowledge becomes more valuable when people can learn from it, question it, rebuild it, improve it, or prove it wrong.

Some work may still require licenses, attribution, intellectual-property protection, or future patent strategy. Protection does not have to mean hoarding. My goal is to protect authorship where necessary while continuing to share as much as responsibly possible.

We share one world. I would rather leave behind tools, lessons, experiments, music, art, code, and questions that someone else can use than lock everything away.

This work is for anyone who **dares to wonder, dares to love, and dares to question the questions**, no matter what they believe.
